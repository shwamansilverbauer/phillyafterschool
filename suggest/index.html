What happens next

  1. Your note lands in a real inbox. A person reads it.
  2. We check it against the program’s own information.
  3. If it holds up, it goes on the school’s page with its source and the date.

Nothing is published automatically, and nobody pays to be listed.

Listings come from each provider’s public pages and are not endorsements. Prices, hours and pickup routes change, so confirm with the provider before you enroll.

Philly After School is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with the School District of Philadelphia or any provider listed.
