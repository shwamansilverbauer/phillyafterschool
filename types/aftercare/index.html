Beehive at Bok

Full days off are booked by the day, and children from any school are welcome. Dates, hours and prices are in its booking page. Aftercare East PassyunkNo dates posted yet

Christian Street YMCA

Full Day School Age Care, 7 am to 6 pm, for kindergarten through 6th grade on days school is closed, with the day split into 45-minute blocks of arts, sports, group games, science and clubs. The Y’s page lists no dates or prices for this school year, so ask the branch which days it will run. AftercareAll-round day campSports & movement Graduate HospitalNo dates posted yet

Dandelion After School

A spring break camp the week the district is off, 9 am to 3 pm, on a sliding scale of $260 to $400 for the week. Kids cook and bake, do crafts and small science experiments, build with recycled materials and walk to nearby playgrounds. Next spring’s dates and sign-up weren’t posted when we checked. Aftercare Passyunk SquareNo dates posted yet

Extended Day, Extended Year at Vare-Washington

The school says its Extended Day, Extended Year program includes a winter break camp, a spring break camp and six weeks of summer camp, free for students at the school. Dates are announced during the year. Aftercare Pennsport and Dickinson NarrowsNo dates posted yet

Kids on 12th

Camp days on teacher work days and most holidays: 8:30 am to 6 pm for enrolled children, 8:30 am to 3 pm for drop-ins. There is no school pickup on those days. On early-release days it picks up and runs 12:30 to 6 pm. Aftercare Washington Square WestNo dates posted yet
