Fleisher Art Memorial

One-day art camps on district days off for ages 5–11: drop-off 8:30 to 9:30 am, classes until 3:30 pm. After Camp Club runs 3:30 to 5:30 pm for an added fee. The price is not on its page. Art & making Bella VistaElection Day · Veterans Day · Presidents’ Day +5 more

Hudson Table

Drop-off cooking classes, posted a few weeks ahead, on some days off. On Veterans Day (November 11) it’s a creative pasta intensive for ages 8–14, 10 am to 1 pm, $95. Art & making Northern LibertiesVeterans Day

NLArts

School’s Out day camps for K–5: hands-on art projects tied to what kids learn in school, and outdoor play, led by teaching artists. $60 for 9 to 3:30 or $75 for 9 to 5:30 (second sibling $50 or $65); 8:30 drop-off is $5. Days this fall are November 3 (sold out), 11, 16 and 25, and a four-day winter break package runs December 28–31 ($200 or $250). Bring a peanut-free lunch, water and snacks. Art & making Northern LibertiesElection Day · Veterans Day · Report card conferences +2 more

Philly Art Center, Queen Village

Full-day vacation camps when public schools are closed, 9 am to 3 pm, for ages 5–12, $98 a day, each day registered separately. Extra care runs 8:15 to 9 am and 3 to 5:30 pm. Art & making Queen VillageElection Day · Veterans Day · Report card conferences +13 more

PlayArts day camps

Full-day camps on 16 days schools are closed in 2026–27, open to any child aged 5–10. 9 am to 3 pm is $80 and 9 am to 6 pm is $110, or $1,120 for all 16 days to 6 pm. No camp on November 25, Martin Luther King Jr. Day or Memorial Day. Financial aid is offered. Art & making South KensingtonElection Day · Veterans Day · Report card conferences +9 more
