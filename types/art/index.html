Hudson Table

Drop-off cooking classes, posted a few weeks ahead, on some days off. On Veterans Day (November 11) it’s a creative pasta intensive for ages 8–14, 10 am to 1 pm, $95. Over winter break it runs full-day drop-off baking camps for ages 8–14, 10 am to 3 pm (flexible drop-off from 9:45, pick-up to 3:15), $130 a day with all food included and nut-free: a snowman cake on December 28, holiday cookies on December 29 and donuts on December 30. TypeArt & makingAreaNorthern LibertiesDays offVeterans Day · Winter break (3 of 8 days)

NLArts

School’s Out day camps for K–5: hands-on art projects tied to what kids learn in school, and outdoor play, led by teaching artists. $60 for 9 to 3:30 or $75 for 9 to 5:30 (second sibling $50 or $65); 8:30 drop-off is $5. Days posted are November 11 (more spots added), 16 (sold out) and 25, then December 21 and 22 before the district’s break, and a four-day winter break package December 28–31 for pre-K 4 to 5th grade ($200 or $250; second sibling $160 or $210). Bring a peanut-free lunch, water and snacks. TypeArt & makingAreaNorthern LibertiesDays offVeterans Day · Report card conferences · Staff professional development +1 more

PlayArts day camps

Full-day camps on 16 days schools are closed in 2026–27, open to any child aged 5–10. 9 am to 3 pm is $80 and 9 am to 6 pm is $110, or $1,120 for all 16 days to 6 pm. No camp on November 25, Martin Luther King Jr. Day or Memorial Day. Financial aid is offered.
