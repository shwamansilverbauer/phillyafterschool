Day camps (4)

Camp TPS at The Philadelphia School

Winter Break Camp December 21–23 and 28–30 (closed December 24, 25, 31 and January 1), 9 am to 4 pm, with week-long themed camps such as ice skating, STEM engineering, a winter wonderland week, gymnastics, clay and cooking, and volleyball. $200 to $450 a week by camp; registration opens October 26, and a $50 late fee applies after November 15. Spring Break Camp runs March 22–26, with the catalog posted later and registration opening November 23. The first two winter days fall before the district’s break. All-round day camp Fitler SquareWinter break (4 of 8 days) · Spring break and Good Friday

Christian Street YMCA

Full Day School Age Care, 7 am to 6 pm, for kindergarten through 6th grade on days school is closed, with the day split into 45-minute blocks of arts, sports, group games, science and clubs. The Y’s page lists no dates or prices for this school year, so ask the branch which days it will run. All-round day campSports & movement Graduate HospitalNo dates posted yet

Elite Sports Factory

School’s Out day camp, 9 am to 3 pm with half-day options, for ages 5–15: sports and games led by counselors, a ninja warrior course and time with friends. Its 2026–27 list covers every district day off, including Thanksgiving week, four days of winter break and Memorial Day, and it follows the Lower Merion calendar too. Prices are on its sign-up page. Sports & movementAll-round day camp ManayunkElection Day · Veterans Day · Report card conferences +14 more

Phield House

Vacation day camps year-round on national holidays, school vacations and snow days, plus early June before summer camp: sports clinics, games and free play indoors in a 25,000-square-foot, climate-controlled building. The page lists no dates, hours or prices; they’re on its sign-up site, or call. Sports & movementAll-round day camp CallowhillNo dates posted yet
