Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Childs pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • The school’s own site gives the school day as 9:00 am to 3:39 pm, pre-kindergarten through 8th grade. It doesn’t mention aftercare; the City’s after-school program locator lists free before and after school care in the building, run by Greater Philadelphia Community Alliance, for students at the school.
  • Childs is the site’s first school in Point Breeze, added because families asked for it. If you know a program that serves it and isn’t here, send it in and it goes on this page.
