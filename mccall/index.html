Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm McCall pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • McCall has a free before and after school program in the building, run by Sunrise of Philadelphia. After school runs until 5:30 pm.
  • Ten outside programs name McCall on their own pickup lists.
  • Options thin out after 5th grade. Zhang Sah takes children through 8th grade, Queen & Rook through age 16, and Butcher’s Sew Shop through 15 (Tuesdays and Thursdays from McCall). Sunrise takes grades 6–8 too.
