    What this roster costs

      An estimate from each program’s published prices, for half a school year: 18 weeks of school, or five monthly bills. It leaves out registration fees, deposits, materials, sibling discounts, subsidies and financial aid. If you know what you’ll pay, add it to any line; it stays on this device. Confirm the price with each program before you budget on it.

      Email it to myself

      Rosters save automatically on this device. The link is the copy you can keep anywhere: anyone who opens it sees this roster, and it’s how you move one to another phone or computer.

      Make it a card

      One picture of the week to text, print, or hand to your child’s teacher, so they know where your child goes each day and who they are.

      The photo never leaves this device. The card is made here in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the photo isn’t saved.

      To email it to a teacher, share or save the card, then attach it to a message from your own email.
