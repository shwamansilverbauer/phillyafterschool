How this list works

  • It’s made from your days-off plan: every day you’ve put a camp on, for every child. A day at home needs no sign-up, so it isn’t here.
  • A tick is your own note. We can’t see whether you registered, and the camp isn’t told anything.
  • Ticks are kept with the plan, on this device and in your profile, so they follow you to another phone or computer.
  • Change the camp on a day and its tick is cleared, because it’s a new sign-up.
