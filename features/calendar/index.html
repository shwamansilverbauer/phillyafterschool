Account features

Calendar & sharing

Build your kids’ plans for the year, see them together in one calendar, and share them with the people who help.

Build it

Build your kids’ week.

Pick an after-school program for each day and each child. It’s kept in your account, so it’s on your phone and your computer.

Plan the days off and the summer.

A camp for every day school is closed, and camps week by week for the summer, for each child.

See it all

Plan the whole year in one place.

Keep track of aftercare, days off, weekends, and summer camps. No spreadsheets required.

Check it on your phone (or fridge).

Grab a calendar file for your phone, or print it out each month.

Share it

Plan it with your co-parent.

You both see and change the same plans, each on your own phone.

Share a week with a sitter or grandparent.

You can stop sharing at any time.

Compare schedules with friends.

Shared schedules are invite-only. Nobody else can find the group or ask to join.

Free for every family

It takes about a minute, and there’s no password to remember.
