Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Marian Anderson pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • The school’s own site names no aftercare of its own; it points to the City’s free Out-of-School Time programs and lists partners. Ask the office which partners run on site after school, and when.
  • One more program names Marian Anderson on its own site but isn’t matched here yet: Philly Music Factory (2100 Chestnut St).
