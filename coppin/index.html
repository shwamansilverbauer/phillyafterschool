Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Coppin pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • Coppin dismisses earlier than most programs start. Ask each provider what time they collect children.
  • Options thin out after 5th grade. For grades 6–8 the pickup choices are Zhang Sah, Makom Community and Butcher’s Sew Shop (Fridays), plus Philly Art Center and the two rec centers through 6th.
  • The city’s free, city-funded after-school programs do not include one at Coppin.
