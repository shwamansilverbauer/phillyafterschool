Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Southwark pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • Southwark has free before and after school care in the building. The school names Sunrise of Philadelphia as its partner, and it is one of the City’s Extended Day, Extended Year schools.
  • The school’s own page names six outside programs that pick up: East Passyunk Rec Center, Beehive, Butcher’s Sew Shop, the Dandelion Project, Philly Art Center and Philly InMovement.
  • Beehive and Philly InMovement’s Bok location are in the Bok building, across Mifflin St from the school.
  • The school lists clubs and teams without days or times. It says intramural sports are before school.
