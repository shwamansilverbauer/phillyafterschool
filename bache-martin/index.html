Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Bache-Martin pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • The school’s own after-school page lists what runs on site and which programs collect children from the school, and it is the source for everything here. It mentions an aftercare in the cafeteria until 5:30 pm on Soccer Shots days without saying who runs it or how to sign up, so ask the office.
  • Bache-Martin is the site’s first school in Fairmount, added because families asked for it. If you know a program that serves it and isn’t here, send it in and it goes on this page.
