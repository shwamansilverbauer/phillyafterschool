Weekend classes in Old City

Saturday and Sunday classes go by where they are. Any child can sign up, whichever school they go to.

Arden Drama School

Saturday acting and musical theatre classes for grades K–8, between 10 am and 4 pm, plus Bookworms for ages 2–5. SaturdayOld CityGrades PK to 8

Close by, in Center City and nearby

Koresh School of Dance

Pre Ballet for ages 4–6 is the one Saturday class for younger children. The other Saturday classes are for ages 16–18. SaturdayRittenhouseGrades PK to 1

MacGuffin Theatre & Film Company

Ages 7–10 rehearse a short show in a production class, then can stay for clowning. Ages 11–18 have drop-in musical theatre and acting at noon and comedy improv in the afternoon. SaturdayRittenhouseGrades 2 to 8

Moore Young Artists Workshop

Ten Saturdays of studio courses. For grades 6–8 this fall: animation, character design, anatomy drawing, fashion illustration and sewing, and drawing and painting. Each course meets in the morning or the afternoon. SaturdayLogan SquareGrades 6 to 8

MoveMakers Philly

Saturday hip hop and breaking classes run 10:15 am to 1 pm at 2100 Chestnut St. Hip hop (K–2nd, and 8 and up) and martial arts and parkour (8 and up) run 10 to 11:30 am at 812 Chestnut St. SaturdayWashington Square WestGrades K to 8

Music Theatre Philly

Grades 1–4: Musical Theatre on Saturdays at 9 am, and Tap, Group Voice and the Finding Nemo Kids musical on Sunday mornings. Grades 5–8: Tap, High School Musical Jr. and Advanced Group Voice on Saturdays, 9 am to 1 pm. SaturdaySundayWashington Square WestGrades 1 to 8

PAFA Saturday Studio Art

A seven-week Saturday drawing class for ages 10–14, taught in PAFA’s studios. SaturdayCenter CityGrades 5 to 8

Philadelphia Museum of Art: Art Kids Classes

Registered Saturday classes for ages 3–4, 5–6, 7–8 and 9–10. Each group meets either late morning or early afternoon. SaturdayFairmountGrades PK to 5

School of Philadelphia Ballet

Creative Movement (ages 3–4), Pre-Ballet (4–5) and Primary (5–6) each meet once a week, on Saturday morning or Sunday midday. Foundations classes for ages 6–7 pair a Saturday with a weekday. SaturdaySundayCenter CityGrades PK to 2

The Franklin Institute: PACTS

Partnerships for Achieving Careers in Technology and Science: hands-on science workshops and mentoring for Philadelphia-area students in grades 6–8, twice a month on Saturday mornings. SaturdayLogan SquareGrades 6 to 8

Walnut Street Theatre School

Acting and musical theatre classes on Saturdays between 9 am and 1:05 pm, in groups for ages 5–7, 8–11 and 12–17. Each ends in a showcase for family and friends. SaturdayWashington Square WestGrades K to 8

ZAZZ

A seven-week fall series: Nutcracker Pre-Ballet for ages 4–6 on Saturdays, and Dance Basics for ages 4–6 on Sundays. A Sunday class for ages 2½ to 3½ runs at the same time. SaturdaySundayRittenhouseGrades PK to 1
