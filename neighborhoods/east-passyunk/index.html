Day camps (4)

Camps on the days district schools are closed, based in East Passyunk. Children from any school can go.

Beehive at Bok

Full days off are booked by the day, and children from any school are welcome. Dates, hours and prices are in its booking page. Aftercare East PassyunkNo dates posted yet

Philly InMovement: MOVE After School

Vacation camps, 9 am to 3:30 pm, for kindergarten and up (plus a pre-K camp for ages 3–5), at Queen Village and Bok, $97.32 a day. Dates are posted through November so far. MOVE After School also still runs 3:30 to 6:30 pm on district closure days, as drop-off. Sports & movementAftercare Queen Village and East PassyunkElection Day · Veterans Day · Report card conferences

Sawubona Creativity Project

One-day theatre camps when schools are closed, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, $60 (its 2025–26 price), for pre-K to 6th grade. Kids improvise dance routines, write skits and learn scenes, and the day ends with a 5:05 pm showcase for families. Theater East PassyunkNo dates posted yet

Sunrise of Philadelphia at Southwark

Sunrise marks Southwark as a site with “mini break” programs, and the City’s program includes free winter-break and spring-break camps for students at the school. Dates are announced during the year. Aftercare East PassyunkNo dates posted yet
