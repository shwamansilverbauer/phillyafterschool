Music (2)

Mister John’s Music

Group piano and guitar classes meet on Saturdays, about 9:30 am to 1:45 pm, and percussion and piano groups on Sundays, about 10 am to 2:30 pm, for roughly ages 4 to 10. Music SaturdaysSundaysBella Vista

Temple Music Prep: Community Music Scholars

Low-cost Saturday morning instrument lessons, theory and ensembles at Temple University Music SaturdaysNorth Philadelphia

Theater (5)

Arden Drama School

Arden Theatre’s school: full-day theater camps on days off, and Saturday classes Theater SaturdaysOld City

MacGuffin Theatre & Film Company

Saturday theatre classes by age group near Rittenhouse Square Theater SaturdaysRittenhouse

Music Theatre Philly

Grades 1–4: Musical Theatre on Saturdays at 9 am, and Tap, Group Voice and the Finding Nemo Kids musical on Sunday mornings. Grades 5–8: Tap, High School Musical Jr. and Advanced Group Voice on Saturdays, 9 am to 1 pm. TheaterMusicSports & movement SaturdaysSundaysWashington Square West

Philly PACK

A theatre and dance school for kids at Federal and Moyamensing: creative movement on Saturdays for ages 3–5, and K–8 classes on weekday afternoons TheaterSports & movement SaturdaysPennsport

Walnut Street Theatre School

Saturday acting and musical theatre classes for kids and teens at the Walnut Street Theatre Theater SaturdaysWashington Square West

Art & making (6)

Fleisher Art Memorial

Young Artists Program classes for ages 5 to 18 meet mainly on Saturdays: drawing and painting, mixed media and sculpture for grades K–5, and other classes for grades 6–12. Art & making SaturdaysBella Vista

MADE Institute: Sunday Sewing

Sunday sewing classes for kids and teens at a West Philly sewing school Art & making SundaysWest Philadelphia

Moore Young Artists Workshop

Three-hour Saturday studio courses for grades 6–12 at Moore College of Art & Design Art & making SaturdaysLogan Square

PAFA Saturday Studio Art

Saturday morning drawing classes for middle schoolers at the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts Art & making SaturdaysCenter City

Philadelphia Museum of Art: Art Kids Classes

Weekly Saturday art classes by age group in the museum’s classrooms Art & making SaturdaysFairmount

Philadelphia’s Magic Gardens: Family Jams

Drop-in family art-making at the mosaic museum on South Street, on the second Sunday of each month Art & making SundaysBella Vista

Blue Chip Flag football, South Philadelphia

A Sunday no-contact flag football league for ages 3 to 17 on the fields at South 10th Street, with a fall and a spring season Sports & movement SundaysPacker Park

Equilibrium Dance Academy

A dance studio on South Broad with ballet, tap, jazz, hip hop and acro from age 3, including Saturday classes Sports & movement SaturdaysNewbold

Fitness Alive: swim lessons in South Philly

Swim lessons for ages 3 and up in the pool at 714 Reed Street, on weekends and weekdays Sports & movement SaturdaysSundaysPassyunk Square

Flips 4 Kidz

Gymnastics for ages 4 to 12 inside East Passyunk Community Center, with three Saturday classes Sports & movement SaturdaysEast Passyunk

Koresh School of Dance

A Saturday morning pre-ballet class for ages 4 to 6 near Rittenhouse Square Sports & movement SaturdaysRittenhouse

MoveMakers Philly

Saturday hip hop and breaking classes run 10:15 am to 1 pm at 2100 Chestnut St. Hip hop (K–2nd, and 8 and up) and martial arts and parkour (8 and up) run 10 to 11:30 am at 812 Chestnut St. Sports & movement SaturdaysWashington Square West

Penn Ice Rink

Sunday learn-to-skate group lessons from age 5 at the University of Pennsylvania’s rink Sports & movement SundaysUniversity City

Pennsport School of Dance

A neighborhood dance school on South 3rd Street: tap, jazz, ballet, hip hop, modern and gymnastics from age 3, with Saturday classes for the youngest Sports & movement SaturdaysWhitman

Philadelphia Martial Arts Academy

Karate for ages 4 to 12 on Jackson Street, with Saturday morning classes Sports & movement SaturdaysWhitman

Philly InMovement: MOVE After School

School-age gymnastics, tumbling and aerials classes for kindergarten and up meet on Saturdays at the Queen Village and Bok gyms, roughly 10 am to 1:45 pm. Sports & movementAftercare SaturdaysQueen Village and East Passyunk

Rizzo Rink

The city’s indoor ice rink on Front Street in Pennsport: free public skating on Sunday afternoons in season, and skating lessons Sports & movementRec centers SundaysPennsport

Starfinder: Saturday soccer

Reading & homework (1)

Kumon Math & Reading Center of South Philadelphia

This covers the programs already on this site, plus weekend-only places from a first sweep of the city. It isn’t every class in Philadelphia, and some listed programs run weekend classes that their sites don’t spell out by day, so ask. Know a weekend class that should be here? Tell us.

Weekend terms start and fill on their own dates, so check with the provider before you count on a class.
