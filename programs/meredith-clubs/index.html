Meredith after-school clubs

Teacher-run clubs at the school: Nature Club, Chess, Science Club, Rock Band, Choir, Arts and Crafts, Debate, Newspaper, Playwriting, Cheer and more. Most meet one afternoon a week.

School clubs, Art & making, Music, STEM, Games, Reading & homework, Sports & movement, Theater, Nature & outdoors In Queen Village Grades K to 8 Checked October 6, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it
