PKK12345678Kindergarten to 6th grade for the before and after school care and the full-day care
Where

Hours
Before school from 7:00 am to the start of the school day, and after school from the end of the school day to 6:00 pm, per the Y’s before-and-after-school page. Full Day School Age Care runs 7 am to 6 pm on school holidays. The branch is open 5 am to 9 pm on weekdays and 7 am to 6 pm on weekends.MoreLess

Cost
Not posted. The Y signs you up for a child care package covering the whole school year plus registration fees, accepts ELRC (child care subsidy) funding, and has financial assistance you apply for before registering.MoreLess
Register
Online. The Y’s page says registration for 2026–27 is open through your Y account online. Call 215-965-4316 to register with a sibling discount, or ask the branch.MoreLess

215-735-5800

The Y’s before-and-after-school page says its before care runs only at this branch, and doesn’t say how children get here from school, so ask the branch. Members get up to two hours of KidZone child watch. The Y’s swim-lesson pages don’t say which branches and days lessons meet, so ask the branch about those too.
