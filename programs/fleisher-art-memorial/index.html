Fleisher Art Memorial

Free drop-in Teen Lounge and weekday art classes for older students

Art & making In Bella Vista Grades 6 to 8 Pick up by 6:00 pm (Teen Lounge) Checked October 5, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it

The details

PKK12345678Weekday options are grades 6–12. K–5 classes meet on Saturdays only.
Where
719 Catharine St
Hours
Teen Lounge: 4–6 pm, Tuesday and Wednesday on school days (one Fleisher page also lists Mondays). Children’s class times vary by term.MoreLess
Days
Tuesday and Wednesday. Teen Lounge only. Children’s classes are mostly on Saturdays.
Pick up by
6:00 pm (Teen Lounge)
Cost
Teen Lounge is free. Weekday classes: $95 base fee per term. Saturday classes: $65 base fee. Help available if cost is a barrier.MoreLess
Days off
One-day art camps on district days off for ages 5–11: drop-off 8:30 to 9:30 am, classes until 3:30 pm. After Camp Club runs 3:30 to 5:30 pm for an added fee. The price is not on its page.MoreLess

Weekends
Young Artists Program classes for ages 5 to 18 meet mainly on Saturdays: drawing and painting, mixed media and sculpture for grades K–5, and other classes for grades 6–12.MoreLess
