Fleisher Art Memorial

Free drop-in Teen Lounge and weekday art classes for older students

Art & making In Bella Vista Grades 6 to 8 Pick up by 6:00 pm (Teen Lounge) Checked October 5, 2026

The details

PKK12345678Weekday options are grades 6–12. K–5 classes meet on Saturdays only.
Where
719 Catharine St
Hours
Teen Lounge: 4–6 pm, Tuesday and Wednesday on school days (one Fleisher page also lists Mondays). Children’s class times vary by term.
Days
Tuesday and Wednesday. Teen Lounge only. Children’s classes are mostly on Saturdays.
Pick up by
6:00 pm (Teen Lounge)
Cost
Teen Lounge is free. Weekday classes: $95 base fee per term. Saturday classes: $65 base fee. Help available if cost is a barrier.
Days off
One-day art camps on district days off for ages 5–11: drop-off 8:30 to 9:30 am, classes until 3:30 pm. After Camp Club runs 3:30 to 5:30 pm for an added fee. The price is not on its page. Dates and details
Register
Online. Teen Lounge needs no registration. Fall 2026 class registration closed September 12.
Next term
Winter term: public registration opens one week after the fall term ends on November 21. Enrolled students get a two-week priority window first. Winter dates are not posted yet.Nov 28: Winter term public registration expected to open. Add to calendar Google Calendar
Contact
215-922-3456
cy@fleisher.org

Nothing on weekday afternoons for K–5. Teen Lounge is for ages 13 and up. Fleisher also runs day-off camps (ages 5–12) when district schools are closed.
