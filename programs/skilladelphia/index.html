Skilladelphia

After-school basketball and lacrosse clinics for girls and non-binary players ages 6–14, Monday to Thursday, with pickup from Meredith, McCall and Nebinger

Runs After school Weekends

Sports & movement In Society Hill Grades 1 to 8 Pick up by 5:30 pm Checked October 19, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it

Follow it, or save it for later

One email when Skilladelphia posts a sign-up date, a deadline or a day-off camp.

Follow means emails when it posts a date. Save to favorites means no emails: it’s kept on your profile so you can find it again.

An email the morning after it posts a date here, then reminders the day before and at about 8 on the morning sign-ups open. Both use a free account.

Old Pine Community Center now goes by The Neighborhub, the program told us in October 2026, so its site may still say either name. It also runs Wednesday evening scrimmages for ages 9–14 ($15 a session), summer camps and clinics, and Monday evening lacrosse at Penn Park in summer. Caregivers can also drop children off from other schools, the program says.
