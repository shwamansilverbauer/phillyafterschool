Movement Callowhill

The climbing gym on Callowhill Street, with single-day, winter-break and spring-break climbing camps for ages 6 to 13

Runs Day camps

Sports & movement In Callowhill Grades 1 to 8 Checked October 16, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it

Follow it, or save it for later

One email when Movement Callowhill posts a sign-up date, a deadline or a day-off camp.

Follow means emails when it posts a date. Save to favorites means no emails: it’s kept on your profile so you can find it again.

An email the morning after it posts a date here, then reminders the day before and at about 8 on the morning sign-ups open. Both use a free account.

Day camps

Camps
Climbing camps for ages 6–13, 9 am to 3 pm, $125 a day: a School Day Off camp on most single-day school holidays, a Winter Break camp on December 23 and 28–31, and a Spring Break camp March 22–26 ($600 for the week). Climbing, teamwork, games and challenges. Its page doesn’t print the year on the break dates, which match this school year’s breaks; its sign-up portal has the exact sessions.MoreLess
