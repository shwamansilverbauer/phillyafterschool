Philadelphia Rock Gym

Climbing camps at its Fishtown and East Falls gyms on days school is closed

Sports & movement In Fishtown, East Falls Grades 1 to 8 Checked October 7, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it

The details

PKK12345678Ages 6–15
Where
2412 E York St (Fishtown) and 3500 Scotts Ln (East Falls)
Hours
Day-off camps run 9 am to 3 pm, or a half day to noon.
Cost
$99 a full day or $60.50 a half day. Early and late care is $20 an hour.
Days off
Kids Day Off camps for ages 6–15, no experience needed: 9 am to 3 pm for $99, or 9 am to noon for $60.50, with snacks and gear included. Early and late care is $20 an hour. You pick the gym and the days when you register.MoreLess
Dates and details

Online. Sign-up links are on the camp page.
