Awbury Arboretum

A 55-acre arboretum in Germantown with unplugged outdoor day camps on eight of the district’s days off

Runs Day camps Summer

Nature & outdoors In Germantown Grades K to 8 Checked October 16, 2026 Claimed by the people who run it

Day camps

Camps
School Day Off Camps, 8:30 am to 3:30 pm, rain or shine: games, hiking, a campfire hot-dog lunch (or bring your own), s’mores, scavenger hunts, crafts and exploring the grounds, with no phones or screens, run by its summer-camp counselors. $75 a day for members, $85 for others, with a $22 scholarship rate for families who qualify.
