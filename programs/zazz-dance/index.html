Follow it, or save it for later

One email when ZAZZ posts a sign-up date, a deadline or a day-off camp.

Follow means emails when it posts a date. Save to favorites means no emails: it’s kept on your profile so you can find it again.

An email the morning after it posts a date here, then reminders the day before and at about 8 on the morning sign-ups open. Both use a free account.

After school

PKK12345678Ages 5–12 after school; the weekend series are for ages 4–6
Hours
3:00–6:00 pm, Monday to Friday, at the Chestnut St studio. Each day brings different classes: acting, improv, musical theater, show choir, dance foundations and technique, acro, tap, hip hop and more. ZAZZ suggests 3 to 5 days a week, but any combination of days works.MoreLess
Days
Monday to Friday
Cost
2026–27: $3,950 a year for 1 or 2 days a week, $4,300 for 3, $4,800 for 4 and $5,200 for 5, in 10 installments, plus a $100 registration fee. Siblings get 10% off. Early pickup from ICS on Wednesdays is $1,200 a year more.MoreLess
Register
Online. Pay the $100 registration fee and fill in the registration form, both on ZAZZ’s after-school page. Families commit to the whole school year; joining mid-year is pro-rated.MoreLess
Contact
215-645-2717

Pickup from each school is guaranteed only with at least four children from it that day. ZAZZ says it will consider a pickup group for other schools if enough families ask, naming Bache-Martin and Stanton among them.

Get the Philly After School app

On an iPhone or iPad, Apple has you add it yourself. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. With an account, log in there once.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Safari first. On an iPhone, the Home Screen copy is also how notifications for the dates you follow reach you.

  1. Open your browser’s menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Chrome first.

  1. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, choose File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

It opens in its own window, from the Dock. Safari on an older Mac doesn’t have this; Chrome or Edge can install it there.

Your browser didn’t offer to install it just now. Most often that means it’s already installed, so look for it with your apps. If not:

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser’s menu and choose “Install Philly After School” (Chrome keeps it under “Cast, save and share”, Edge under “Apps”).

It’s this same site, kept like an app: free, nothing from an app store, and it opens right to your plans.
