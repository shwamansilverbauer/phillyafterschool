Log in to your accountYour account

This is where all that fun planning stuff we talked about is. Remember that stuff? New here? Create a free account

Update your name, email address and phone number, your school and grades, what you’re emailed about, what you follow, and who you share with (if you want to).

Family admin, streamlined.

  • Plan the whole year in one place. Keep track of aftercare, days off, weekends, and summer camps. No spreadsheets required.
  • Check it on your phone (or fridge). Grab a calendar file for your phone, or print it out each month.
  • Never miss a sign-up. Follow your school and favorite programs, and we'll remind you when sign-ups open.
  • Share a week with a sitter or grandparent. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Compare schedules with friends. Shared schedules are invite-only. Nobody else can find the group or ask to join.

Making an account means you agree to the terms of use.

Run a program or camp? Claim your listing. It’s the same account.

You make the plans. You decide who sees them.

  • Your profile keeps the plans you make: a child’s first name with their week, days off and summer, and your school.
  • A shared week opens only for the email address you sent it to, once that person has signed in. It can’t be searched for, and its link shows nothing to anyone else.
  • You can take a week back, stop sharing, or delete your account at any time. Shared weeks delete themselves when the school year ends.

The full details are on the privacy page.
