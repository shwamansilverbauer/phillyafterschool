School’s closed. What now?

When school is out for a holiday, find who runs a camp or a full day. Filter by category, neighborhood, and find a class for every day.

Who runs something when school is closed

38 listed programs say they run camps or full days on days off. Children from any school can go, so start from where you live. 17 of them had no dates on their site when we checked, so ask which days they cover.
