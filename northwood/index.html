Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Northwood pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • Northwood’s 2026–27 handbook says the school partners with CORA Services and BayWay Cares for before- and after-school care. The City’s program locator lists CORA’s free after-school program (YouthCOR) at the school, 3:00 to 6:00 pm, for kindergarten to 8th grade. It has a listing here. The handbook doesn’t say where BayWay Cares’ care meets or what it costs, so ask the office.
  • Northwood’s own site lists its clubs and sports: soccer, baseball, softball, basketball, cross country, cheerleading and track, and clubs from a Percy Jackson book club to gardening, magic and Dungeons & Dragons. They have a listing here. Families sign up on forms sent home, and the current list is in the weekly Bulldog Blast newsletter.
  • Northwood keeps its own calendar, not the District’s. Its days off on this page come from its 2026–27 academic calendar: it closes for Yom Kippur and Eid al-Fitr, on professional learning days (October 9, December 23 and May 14), and its spring break is March 22 to 26 and March 29. The days-off planner and the list of who’s open go by the District’s, so check a camp’s dates against Northwood’s.
  • The calendar marks dismissal at 12:00 pm every Wednesday, on the conference days (December 10–11 and March 18–19), through the state testing weeks in late April and early May, and on the last day, June 11. Ask CORA whether it starts early on those days.
  • If you know a program that serves Northwood and isn’t here, send it in and it goes on this page.

Get the Philly After School app

On an iPhone or iPad, Apple has you add it yourself. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. With an account, log in there once.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Safari first. On an iPhone, the Home Screen copy is also how notifications for the dates you follow reach you.

  1. Open your browser’s menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Chrome first.

  1. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, choose File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

It opens in its own window, from the Dock. Safari on an older Mac doesn’t have this; Chrome or Edge can install it there.

Your browser didn’t offer to install it just now. Most often that means it’s already installed, so look for it with your apps. If not:

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser’s menu and choose “Install Philly After School” (Chrome keeps it under “Cast, save and share”, Edge under “Apps”).

It’s this same site, kept like an app: free, nothing from an app store, and it opens right to your plans.
