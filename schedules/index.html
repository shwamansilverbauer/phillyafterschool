Build a schedule

Three planners, one for each part of a family’s year: the school week, the days school is closed, and the summer. All three are free and work without an account.

Monday to Friday

Build your week

Put a program on each afternoon and see the whole week in one place. More than one child? Each gets their own.

  • Add programs day by day, or roll a themed week to start from
  • See what the week comes to, from each program’s published prices
  • Turn it into one picture to text, print, or hand to a teacher

Build your day-camp plan

Every day district schools are closed this year, and the 20 programs that run a camp on them.

  • See who’s open on each day off, break by break
  • Pick a camp, or a day at home, for each child
  • Save it as a calendar picture, or put the days in your phone’s calendar

Build your summer

The weeks between the last day of school and Labor Day, and which of 44 camps run in each one.

  • See on one chart which camps run each week
  • Line up a camp for every week, child by child, and see what it adds up to
  • Make it a calendar picture, or put the weeks in your phone’s calendar

My kids’ calendar

The three plans above, added up: the school week, every day off, the camp weeks and the sign-up dates, in date order for the whole year.

  • One calendar file for your phone, which you can bring up to date later
  • Room for your own dates: picture day, pretzel day, a form that’s due
  • A picture of any month to send or print
  • Private: it has no link, and only you can open it

How the planners work

  • Nothing to put on a plan yet? Find your school to see what works with it, or look through every program.
  • A plan stays on the phone or computer you made it on. Nothing about your child is sent to us.
  • With a free account you can keep each plan in your profile, so it’s on every device you sign in on. Create a free account
  • Days, hours and prices come from each program’s own website. Check with the program before you count on a spot.
