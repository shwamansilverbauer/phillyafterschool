Welcome to Philly After School. You have an account now, which means the school week, the days off and the summer can all live in one place, on your phone and your laptop, with no password to remember.

Three things worth doing first:

1Save your school and your kids’ grades

Every list on the site then starts from the programs that take your kids. It’s at the top of your account.

2Build your week

Drag after-school programs onto the days, see what the week costs, and turn it into a card for the fridge or the group chat.

3Follow your school

We email you the day before sign-ups open and the morning of, for your school and any program or camp you follow. It’s one tap on your school’s page.

One rule we keep: everything on a listing comes from the program’s own website (or from the program itself), and each listing says when it was last checked. If something looks wrong, every listing has a “Suggest an update” link, and a real person reads it.
