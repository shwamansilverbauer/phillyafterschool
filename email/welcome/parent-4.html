What keeps this site going

Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

If you’ve already chipped in: thank you, and you can stop reading here.

Two weeks in, a straight answer to a fair question: how does a free site keep running? Philly After School is free for parents and will stay that way, and free programs are always listed free. What it costs is hosting, the email service that sends you sign-up reminders, and the hours spent reading program websites so you don’t have to. Right now two parents cover that.

If the site has saved you an evening, a few dollars a month helps it keep going, once or monthly, whatever you like. It changes nothing about what you see: nobody is ranked higher, nobody is featured, and the site stays the same for everyone whether they give or not.

Help the site keep going

Josh and Rachael
Philly After School
