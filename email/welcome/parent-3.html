The card

One picture of the week, first names and days only, for the group chat or the fridge. “Make it a card” draws it; save it, print it or send it.

Compare schedules with friends

Invite a few families by email and see each other’s weeks: who’s at the rec center on Tuesdays, who could split a pickup. Groups are invite-only; nobody can find one or ask to join. Groups
