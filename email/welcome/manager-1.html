Hi {{ first_name|default:"there" }},

Thanks for making a manager account. Your program’s listing is already on the site and parents are already reading it; claiming it makes it yours to keep right.

1Claim it with your work email

An address at your program’s own website claims it on the spot. Any other address: reply to this email and we’ll hand it to your account. Claim your listing

2Say whether there’s space

Mark the listing “Spots open”, “Waitlist” or “Full”; parents see it with the date, and it comes down after 30 days unless you set it again.

3Post your dates and send changes

Sign-up openings, deadlines, term dates and camp days, as soon as you set them: families who follow you get an email the day before and the morning of. A new price, new hours or a new pickup school goes the same way; a person reads it and the listing follows, usually within a few days.

4Your numbers

Once a month: how often the listing was opened, how many clicks went on to your sign-up page, how many families put you on a plan. Numbers only, never who.

Listings are free and stay free. If you’d like photos and your own words on yours, there’s a premium listing; the link is on your listing’s tools, and the next email shows what it adds.
