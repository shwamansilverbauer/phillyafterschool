Your listing is free, and it stays free. Premium adds your photos, your words and your offers to it, and shows you what families do with it. Here is a premium listing, with made-up names:

1Photos and your logo

Up to six photos and a logo, at the top of your listing.

2An offer or event line

An open house, a trial class, early-bird pricing. It shows near the top until the date you set.

3A section in your own words

Say what makes your program yours, the way you’d say it to a parent on a tour.

4A button for questions

Parents send a question from your listing and it lands in your inbox, with their address to reply to.

5Fuller numbers, and a flyer to print

Month by month: how often your listing was opened, how many families follow it or saved it, and which schools they come from. Numbers only, never who. Plus a flyer with a code that opens your listing, ready to print.

It’s $19 a month or $190 a year. That’s the founding price, for the first programs to sign up, and it stays yours for as long as you stay signed up; later, premium will cost $29 a month or $290 a year. Nothing about premium changes where a listing sits: nobody is ranked higher or featured.
