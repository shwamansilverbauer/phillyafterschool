How reviews work

Reviews on Philly After School are written by parents and caregivers about their own family’s experience. Here is what is allowed, how they’re checked, and how one comes down.

Who can write one

  • A parent or caregiver, about a program their own child went to. A review can also be in a child’s own words, typed by their parent.
  • Each review comes from an account whose email address has been confirmed with a code. One review per program from each account.
  • The people who run a program, and people at another program, can’t review it.

What’s allowed

  • What your family experienced: pickup, the activities, homework help, how your child got on, whether you’d sign up again.
  • Fair criticism. A low rating is welcome when it’s first-hand.

What isn’t

  • Names of other children or of individual staff.
  • Contact details or links.
  • Strong language.
  • Accusations of a crime, abuse, unsafe conditions, discrimination or a licensing problem. A review isn’t the place for these: tell the program and the proper authorities, and write to us at <a href="mailto:contact@phillyafterschool.org">contact@phillyafterschool.org</a>.
  • Anything written for someone else, paid for, or offered in exchange for something.

How reviews are checked

  • Every review is checked against these rules before it goes up. One that may break a rule, or comes from an account that may not be first-hand, is read by a person first.
  • Ratings and words are published as written, or not at all. We don’t edit reviews.
  • Nobody pays to have a review posted, hidden or removed, and a program’s premium listing has no say over its reviews.

How a program answers

  • A program that has claimed its listing is told when a new review of it is published.
  • A program can’t remove or change a review. If it thinks one breaks these rules, or isn’t about its program, it can write to us at <a href="mailto:contact@phillyafterschool.org">contact@phillyafterschool.org</a>, and we’ll look at the review against these rules.

How a review comes down

  • You can ask for your own review to be changed or taken down at any time: reply to the email we sent when it went up, or write to <a href="mailto:contact@phillyafterschool.org">contact@phillyafterschool.org</a>.
  • We take down a review that breaks these rules, including after it has been published.
  • Deleting your account doesn’t take your review down by itself. Ask us and we will.

Get the Philly After School app

On an iPhone or iPad, Apple has you add it yourself. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. With an account, log in there once.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Safari first. On an iPhone, the Home Screen copy is also how notifications for the dates you follow reach you.

  1. Open your browser’s menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Chrome first.

  1. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, choose File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

It opens in its own window, from the Dock. Safari on an older Mac doesn’t have this; Chrome or Edge can install it there.

Your browser didn’t offer to install it just now. Most often that means it’s already installed, so look for it with your apps. If not:

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser’s menu and choose “Install Philly After School” (Chrome keeps it under “Cast, save and share”, Edge under “Apps”).

It’s this same site, kept like an app: free, nothing from an app store, and it opens right to your plans.
