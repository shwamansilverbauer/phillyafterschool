Kid’s CornerKid’s Corner

Every class and camp your child goes to earns them something on a page of their own: shiny pins that grow grander as they level up, a cartoon map of Philly with surprises and secret spots to find, a bulb of their own to dress up, and showdowns where they pick the class they had more fun at. Eddie Bulboa, the bulb in our logo, shows them around.

Hand over the phone. Kid’s Corner is for the kid; the grown-ups’ corner is behind a press-and-hold.

For kids

A corner just for you!

Ask a grown-up to make a free account, and every class and camp you go to earns you cool stuff on a page of your own. Eddie Bulboa, the bulb in our logo, shows you around.

  • Earn pins

    Every class gets you a pin. Keep going and it turns bronze, then silver, then gold!

  • Be a lightbulb

    Pick your bulb, then dress it up as you level up: shades, a cape, a crown, even a jetpack.

  • Explore Philly

    Your classes clear the clouds on a map of Philly, with surprises and secret spots to find.

  • Pick your favorite

    Which class was more fun? You decide, and your grown-up sees it first.

Grown-ups: make a free account

For grown-ups: it’s free, you say which classes count, and nothing your child does there is counted or sent anywhere.

How it works

  • A page for each child on your account, by first name. No child signs in and nothing is typed by a child: it’s the parent’s account, handed over for a look. Each child picks a lightbulb to be.
  • What counts is what you confirm. The page suggests from your kept week, your summer and your days off (“Did Sam go to chess club this year?”), and you say yes or no. You can add anything the plans missed.
  • From that come pins for each kind of program, each season and the neighborhoods, which go from bronze to silver to gold and get grander each time; a Repeater Reward for going back to a class they love (each day a week, each term, each week of the same camp); a level that goes up and unlocks things for their bulb to wear; a map of Philadelphia whose clouds clear where they have been, with a surprise or two in each neighborhood and secret spots that turn up as they go to more classes nearby; and showdowns where they pick which class they had more fun at, two at a time, and the ones they pick come first when you plan the next term.
  • Nothing on this page is counted, recorded or sent anywhere. It has no link of its own to share: a picture of the pins, with no name and no map, is the only thing that leaves it.
