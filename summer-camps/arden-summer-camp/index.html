Tell me when it posts summer 2027

One email when Arden Drama School posts its 2027 dates and prices, and one before sign-ups open if it names a day.

An email the morning after we see it, then reminders the day before and at about 8 on the morning sign-ups open. We read each camp’s site again about once a month, so the first one can come a few weeks after the camp’s own announcement. If a camp fills fast, watch its site too. Following uses a free account. A favorite sends no email: it’s kept in your account so you can find it again.

Dates, prices and openings change, and many camps fill early. Confirm with the camp before you plan around a week.
