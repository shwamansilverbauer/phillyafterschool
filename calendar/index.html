Getting your calendar…

The year

Your plan

Your calendar fills in from your planners, and from any dates you add here yourself.

    Add your own dates

    Picture day, pretzel day, a form that’s due, a half day: anything you need to remember, for one child or for everyone. They show up on the calendar, in the pictures and in the file for your phone. Something that happens every week needs only the day of the week.

    Who it’s for
    Add several at once

    Type or paste one on each line, starting with the date or the weekday. They go to whoever is picked under “Who it’s for”.
