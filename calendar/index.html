Getting your calendar…

The year

1Add it

Your calendar fills in from your planners, and from any dates you add here yourself.

    Add your own dates

    Picture day, pretzel day, a form that’s due, a half day: anything you need to remember, for one child or for everyone. They show up in the calendar below, in the pictures and in the file for your phone. Something that happens every week needs only the day of the week.

    Who it’s for
    Add several at once

    Type or paste one on each line, starting with the date or the weekday. They go to whoever is picked under “Who it’s for”.

      A weekly one repeats until the last day of school and skips the days school is closed. Your own dates are kept on this device only: they aren’t sent to us, so add them on the phone or computer you’ll look at.

      3Share it

      Make it a picture

      A calendar for each month, to print for the fridge or send to family. It’s made here in your browser.

      The photo never leaves this device. The calendar is made here in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the photo isn’t saved.

      4Pencil it in

      Add it to your calendar

      One file with everything ticked below, for the calendar on your phone or computer. Open it and your calendar app adds the entries. It’s a copy: if a date changes later, this page will say so, and you download it again.
