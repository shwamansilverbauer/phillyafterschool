My kids’ calendar

Everything you’ve planned for the school year and the summer, in one place: the after-school week, every day off, camp weeks and sign-up dates. Only you can see it.

The whole year, lined up

Sign in and what you’ve planned comes together on one page, on any device.

  • Every day school is closed, with where each child will be.
  • The summer, week by week.
  • Sign-up openings and deadlines for the programs and camps you chose.
  • Your own dates: picture day, pretzel day, a form that’s due.
  • A calendar picture for each month, and a file that puts it all in your phone’s calendar.
Create a free accountLog in

It’s private. There is no link to your calendar and no way to share it from here: a picture or a file you save is yours to send.

Planning doesn’t need an account: build a week, plan the days off, plan the summer.

Getting your calendar…

The year

    Add your own dates

    Picture day, pretzel day, a form that’s due, a half day: anything you need to remember, for one child or for everyone. They show up in the calendar below, in the pictures and in the file for your phone. Something that happens every week needs only the day of the week.

    Who it’s for
    Add several at once

    Type or paste one on each line, starting with the date or the weekday. They go to whoever is picked under “Who it’s for”.

      A weekly one repeats until the last day of school and skips the days school is closed. Your own dates are kept on this device only: they aren’t sent to us, so add them on the phone or computer you’ll look at.

      Put it in your phone’s calendar

      One file with everything ticked below. Open it and your calendar app adds the entries. It’s a copy: if a date changes later, this page will say so, and you download it again.

      What goes in the file

      Make it a picture

      A calendar for each month, to print for the fridge or send to family. It’s made here in your browser.

      The photo never leaves this device. The calendar is made here in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the photo isn’t saved.
