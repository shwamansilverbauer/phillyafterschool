Run a program or camp? Claim your listing.

Find your program below and claim it with your work email. Then keep its dates, costs and hours up to date yourself. It’s free, and it takes a couple of minutes.

What claiming gets you

Your listing is already on the site, and parents are already reading it. Claim it and it becomes yours to keep right.

  1. 1Keep your dates current. Post sign-up openings, deadlines, term dates and camp days as soon as you set them, instead of waiting for us to find them.
  2. 2Say whether there’s space. Mark your listing “Spots open”, “Waitlist” or “Full”. It shows to parents straight away, with the date.
  3. 3See your numbers. How many times your listing was opened this month, how many clicks went on to your sign-up page and website, and how many families put you on a plan.
  4. 4Fix your costs and hours. When a price or a pickup time changes, send it once and the listing follows.
  5. 5Show parents it’s kept up. A “Claimed” mark tells them the listing is looked after by the people who run it.

How claiming works

  1. Find your listing. Search by name above. Every program and summer camp on the site is there.
  2. Sign in with your work email. It has to be an address at your program’s own website: if the listing’s website is example.org, an address ending in @example.org. That is how we know you speak for it. If the addresses match, the claim stands straight away.
  3. Send changes when something moves. New dates, a new price, new hours. We read each one and update the listing, usually within a few days.

Good to know

  • A personal address (Gmail, Yahoo and so on) can’t claim a listing, even if it’s the one printed on your flyer. We have no way to tell it apart from anyone else’s.
  • “Continue with Google” works if your work email is a Google account at your program’s address. A personal Gmail account will sign you in, but it can’t claim anything.
  • Some listings sit on a website many people share: a city rec center, a school’s clubs, a program inside a university. Those claims wait for us to say yes, and we may write to ask a question first.
  • Parents see a “Claimed” mark on the listing. Your name and email address are never shown.
  • A claim doesn’t change what the listing says by itself. Changes you send are looked at by a person, then published.
  • Listings are free and stay free. Nobody pays to be listed or to be ranked higher. Paid extras, such as a photo at the top of your listing, are planned for later.
  • Not on the site yet? Suggest your program or your camp, with its website, and claim it once it’s up.

What we keep about a claim is on the privacy page.
