Want more on your listing?

A premium listing adds photos and your logo, a section in your own words, an offer or event line, a button parents use to send you a question, and month-by-month numbers. The listing itself stays free; premium is the extras.

How it works

  1. Find your listing. Search by name above. Every program and summer camp on the site is there.
  2. Sign in with your work email. If it matches your program’s website (you@example.org for example.org), you’ll be verified instantly. Using a different address? Email contact@phillyafterschool.org from it. Once we verify it's listed on your program’s website, we’ll get you set up.
  3. Keep us updated. New dates, new price, new hours? The "update" tab in your listing lets you notify a real person when program info changes. We review all submitted updates before publishing them, usually within a few days.
