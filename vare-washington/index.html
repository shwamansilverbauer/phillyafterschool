Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Vare-Washington pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • Vare-Washington has free before and after school care in the building, through the City’s Extended Day, Extended Year program. The City’s page says registration is closed for now, so ask the school office about openings.
  • Dismissal is 2:54 pm, earlier than at most schools nearby. Most outside programs start at 3:30, so ask each one when its staff collect.
  • For grades 6–8, Zhang Sah takes children through 8th grade and Queen & Rook through age 16. Ask the other programs about older children. Fleisher is about half a mile away for children old enough to walk.
