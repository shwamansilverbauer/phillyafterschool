Kids on 12th

Aftercare

Hours
8:30 am to 6 pm (drop-ins to 3 pm)
What it runs
Camp days on teacher work days and most holidays: 8:30 am to 6 pm for enrolled children, 8:30 am to 3 pm for drop-ins. There is no school pickup on those days. On early-release days it picks up and runs 12:30 to 6 pm. Its 2026–27 calendar marks camp days by school; the dates here are the district’s. It closes at 4 pm on November 25 and December 23, and is closed Nov 26–27, Dec 24–25, Dec 31 and Jan 1.
Where
1112 Chestnut St, Suite C
On school days
Picks up from Coppin and McCall.

The Schuylkill Center for Environmental Education

Nature & outdoors

Hours
8 am to 3 pm; aftercare 3 to 6 pm
Cost
$80 a day for members, $90 for others; aftercare $20
Ages
5–12
What it runs
Day-off camps, 8 am to 3 pm, for grades K–6: nature explorations led by its educators, using the science lab, Discovery Center, trails, streams and ponds, in light rain and snow. $80 a day for members, $90 for others; aftercare 3 to 6 is $20 more. Its 2026–27 schedule covers most of the district’s days off (not Thanksgiving week, winter break or Memorial Day) and some days other districts are off. Registration closes a week before each day, and a day is cancelled a week out if too few sign up.
Where
8480 Hagy’s Mill Rd
