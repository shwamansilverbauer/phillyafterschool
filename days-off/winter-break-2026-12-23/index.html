Days off from school

Winter break, December 23 to January 1: camps for the break

Philadelphia’s district schools are closed from December 23 to January 1. 9 programs listed here have posted a camp for it.

School year 2026–27 Calendar checked October 5, 2026 8 weekdays off 9 camps posted

Open during the break

A program is listed here only when its own website names this date. Spots fill, so check with the program before you count on one.

Arden Drama School

Theater

What it runs
Play-in-a-Day for grades K–5: the group makes up and performs its own play, 9 am to 5 pm (9 to 4 on December 31). One-day musical theatre workshops for grades 6–8 run 9:30 am to 4:30 pm on the same days through December 29. Financial aid is available.
Days it has posted
Dec 28, Dec 29, Dec 30, Dec 31, 4 of the 8 weekdays.

Camp TPS at The Philadelphia School

All-round day camp

What it runs
Winter Break Camp December 21–23 and 28–30 (closed December 24, 25, 31 and January 1), 9 am to 4 pm, with week-long themed camps such as ice skating, STEM engineering, a winter wonderland week, gymnastics, clay and cooking, and volleyball. $200 to $450 a week by camp; registration opens October 26, and a $50 late fee applies after November 15. Spring Break Camp runs March 22–26, with the catalog posted later and registration opening November 23. The first two winter days fall before the district’s break.
Days it has posted
Dec 23, Dec 28, Dec 29, Dec 30, 4 of the 8 weekdays.
Where
2501 Lombard St

Movement Callowhill

Sports & movement

What it runs
Climbing camps for ages 6–13, 9 am to 3 pm, $125 a day: a School Day Off camp on most single-day school holidays, a Winter Break camp on December 23 and 28–31, and a Spring Break camp March 22–26 ($600 for the week). Climbing, teamwork, games and challenges. Its page doesn’t print the year on the break dates, which match this school year’s breaks; its sign-up portal has the exact sessions.

NLArts

Art & making

What it runs
School’s Out day camps for K–5: hands-on art projects tied to what kids learn in school, and outdoor play, led by teaching artists. $60 for 9 to 3:30 or $75 for 9 to 5:30 (second sibling $50 or $65); 8:30 drop-off is $5. Days this fall are November 3 (sold out), 11, 16 and 25, and a four-day winter break package runs December 28–31 ($200 or $250). Bring a peanut-free lunch, water and snacks.
