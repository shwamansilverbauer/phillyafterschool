Kids on 12th

Aftercare

Hours
8:30 am to 6 pm (drop-ins to 3 pm)
What it runs
Camp days on teacher work days and most holidays: 8:30 am to 6 pm for enrolled children, 8:30 am to 3 pm for drop-ins. There is no school pickup on those days. On early-release days it picks up and runs 12:30 to 6 pm. Its 2026–27 calendar marks camp days by school; the dates here are the district’s. It closes at 4 pm on November 25 and December 23, and is closed Nov 26–27, Dec 24–25, Dec 31 and Jan 1.
Where
1112 Chestnut St, Suite C
On school days
Picks up from Coppin, McCall, Independence Charter and Greenfield.

NLArts

Art & making

Hours
9 am to 3:30 pm, or to 5:30 pm
Cost
$60 to 3:30, $75 to 5:30; second sibling $50 or $65
Ages
5–11
What it runs
School’s Out day camps for K–5: hands-on art projects tied to what kids learn in school, and outdoor play, led by teaching artists. $60 for 9 to 3:30 or $75 for 9 to 5:30 (second sibling $50 or $65); 8:30 drop-off is $5. Days posted are November 11 (more spots added), 16 (sold out) and 25, then December 21 and 22 before the district’s break, and a four-day winter break package December 28–31 for pre-K 4 to 5th grade ($200 or $250; second sibling $160 or $210). Bring a peanut-free lunch, water and snacks.
