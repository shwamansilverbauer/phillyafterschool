Election Day, Tuesday, November 3: camps for the day off

Philadelphia’s district schools are closed on Tuesday, November 3. 15 programs listed here have posted a camp for it.

Awbury Arboretum

Nature & outdoors

What it runs
School Day Off Camps, 8:30 am to 3:30 pm, rain or shine: games, hiking, a campfire hot-dog lunch (or bring your own), s’mores, scavenger hunts, crafts and exploring the grounds, with no phones or screens, run by its summer-camp counselors. $75 a day for members, $85 for others, with a $22 scholarship rate for families who qualify. Listed for Election Day, Veterans Day, Presidents’ Day, Eid al-Fitr and both May days off.

The Schuylkill Center for Environmental Education

Nature & outdoors

What it runs
Day-off camps, 8 am to 3 pm, for grades K–6: nature explorations led by its educators, using the science lab, Discovery Center, trails, streams and ponds, in light rain and snow. $80 a day for members, $90 for others; aftercare 3 to 6 is $20 more. Its 2026–27 schedule covers most of the district’s days off (not Thanksgiving week, winter break or Memorial Day) and some days other districts are off. Registration closes a week before each day, and a day is cancelled a week out if too few sign up.
