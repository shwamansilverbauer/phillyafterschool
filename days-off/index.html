School’s closed. Now what?

Every day district schools are closed this year, who runs a camp on each, and a plan you can build for each child.

Your days off

Choose where each child will be on each day school is closed. Your picks are saved on this device and nowhere else.

Stays on this device.

    Make it a calendar

    One picture of every day off, or a calendar for each month, to print for the fridge or send to a sitter. Every child with a day planned is on it.

    The photo never leaves this device. The calendar is made here in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the photo isn’t saved.

    Get a heads-up before each day off

    An email at least 10 days ahead, with the listed programs running a camp that day.

    Sign-up dates reach you the morning after they’re posted here, then again the day before and at about 8 that morning. Days off come in a Sunday round-up. Following uses a free account.

    Before you count on it

    • Expect to drop your child off. Several programs say there is no school pickup on a day school is closed.
    • A camp day is usually booked and paid for on its own, separate from after-school.
    • The district’s calendar lists no early-dismissal days for students this year. If your school sends children home early, ask the program what it does.
    • Charter schools keep their own calendars, so their days off can differ from these.
    • Know a program that runs on days off and isn’t here? Tell us.
