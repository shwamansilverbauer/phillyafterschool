Memorial Day: camps for the day off

Philadelphia’s district schools are closed on Monday, May 31. One program listed here has posted a camp for it.

Open that day

A program is listed here only when its own website names this date. Spots fill, so check with the program before you count on one.

Elite Sports Factory

Sports & movementAll-round day camp

Hours
9 am to 3 pm, with half-day options
Ages
5–15
What it runs
School’s Out day camp, 9 am to 3 pm with half-day options, for ages 5–15: sports and games led by counselors, a ninja warrior course and time with friends. Its 2026–27 list covers every district day off, including Thanksgiving week, four days of winter break and Memorial Day, and it follows the Lower Merion calendar too. Prices are on its sign-up page.

5050 Umbria St
