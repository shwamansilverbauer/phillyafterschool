A passport for every kidPassport

Every class and camp your child has been to becomes a stamp on a page of their own: stamps for the kinds of program, the neighborhoods, the milestones; a map of every place; a bracket to pick favorites.

Hand over the phone. The passport is for the kid; the grown-ups’ corner is behind a press-and-hold.

How it works

  • A page for each child on your account, by first name. No child signs in and nothing is typed by a child: it’s the parent’s account, handed over for a look.
  • What counts is what you confirm. The page suggests from your kept week, your summer and your days off (“Did Sam go to chess club this year?”), and you say yes or no. You can add anything the plans missed.
  • Stamps are worked out from that: one for each kind of program, each neighborhood, the milestones (1, 5, 10, 25) and the seasons. A map shows every place. A bracket lets the kid pick favorites, and those come first when you plan the next term.
  • Nothing on this page is counted, recorded or sent anywhere. It has no link of its own to share: a picture of the stamps, with no name and no map, is the only thing that leaves it.
