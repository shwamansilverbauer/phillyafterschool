Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Lingelbach pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • The school’s own site says the school day ends promptly at 2:54 pm, that children should not be dropped off before 8:10 am, and that it has no before-care. It names no aftercare of its own.
  • Lingelbach is the site’s first school in Germantown, added because families asked for it. If you know a program that serves it and isn’t here, send it in and it goes on this page.

Get the Philly After School app

On an iPhone or iPad, Apple has you add it yourself. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. With an account, log in there once.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Safari first. On an iPhone, the Home Screen copy is also how notifications for the dates you follow reach you.

  1. Open your browser’s menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Chrome first.

  1. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, choose File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

It opens in its own window, from the Dock. Safari on an older Mac doesn’t have this; Chrome or Edge can install it there.

Your browser didn’t offer to install it just now. Most often that means it’s already installed, so look for it with your apps. If not:

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser’s menu and choose “Install Philly After School” (Chrome keeps it under “Cast, save and share”, Edge under “Apps”).

It’s this same site, kept like an app: free, nothing from an app store, and it opens right to your plans.
