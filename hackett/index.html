PlayArts AfterSchool

Aftercare inside Adaire, Hackett and Moffet with daily outdoor play, art lessons and homework help

AftercareArt & making

PKK12345678Kindergarten to 5th grade; Pre-K too at Hackett and Moffet (PlayArts says it has no Pre-K space at Adaire)

From dismissal to 6:00 pm on school days. PlayArts says the 2026–27 price also covers any early-dismissal day, from noon at the school.

$445 a month for 2026–27 (kindergarten to 5th grade), covering every regular program day, 3:00 to 6:00 pm. A nonrefundable deposit of one month’s tuition holds a spot and counts as the last payment. Pre-K at Moffet is $585 a month with Fridays and early dismissals, or $370 a month Monday to Thursday; at Hackett, Pre-K is $585 a month with Fridays and early dismissals, or $175 a month for the early-dismissal Fridays only.

PlayArts runs its after-school program at Adaire, Hackett and Moffet. At Adaire, children are dismissed by their teachers and meet PlayArts staff by the auditorium; at Hackett, kindergarten to 5th graders go to the cafetorium to meet PlayArts teachers and Pre-K children are collected from their classrooms; at Moffet, PlayArts staff collect Pre-K to 2nd graders from their classrooms. Parents keep in touch through the Remind app. PlayArts’ own Hackett page gives the daily schedule: dismissal at 3:15–3:30, snack in the cafetorium, outdoor play on the Hackett playground, then art, homework and games inside, with pickup from the playground (or the main doors on York Street in bad weather) until 6:00 pm. Hackett’s site lists PlayArts too.

Before you enroll

  • Pickup lists change every year, and most providers need a minimum number of children from a school. Ask each one to confirm Hackett pickup for the days you need.
  • Ask what happens on early-dismissal days and district days off.
  • City rec centers post each year’s after-school listing late, so call before counting on a price or a spot. Their listings live in the Parks & Rec finder.
  • Free, city-funded programs are in the city’s After School and Summer Program Locator.
  • Hackett’s own site lists these after-school programs: After-School All-Stars, Cione Recreation Center, First Class Learning Academy, PlayArts, Shissler Recreation Center and STEM Prep Academy.
  • PlayArts runs its after-school program inside Hackett, from dismissal to 6:00 pm, for Pre-K to 5th grade. It has a listing here.
  • Shissler Recreation Center picks up Hackett students, according to the City’s program data, and Hackett names it too. It has a listing here.
  • The others Hackett names are under “Also named on Hackett’s site”: we couldn’t confirm on their own pages or the City’s that they serve Hackett.
  • Hackett’s own clubs (the Green Team, choir, band, strings, the Page Turners, Movement Makers and more) have a listing here.
  • If you know a program that serves Hackett and isn’t here, send it in and it goes on this page.

Get the Philly After School app

On an iPhone or iPad, Apple has you add it yourself. It takes three taps:

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. With an account, log in there once.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Safari first. On an iPhone, the Home Screen copy is also how notifications for the dates you follow reach you.

  1. Open your browser’s menu (the three dots).
  2. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Reading this inside Instagram or Facebook? Open the page in Chrome first.

  1. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, choose File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

It opens in its own window, from the Dock. Safari on an older Mac doesn’t have this; Chrome or Edge can install it there.

Your browser didn’t offer to install it just now. Most often that means it’s already installed, so look for it with your apps. If not:

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser’s menu and choose “Install Philly After School” (Chrome keeps it under “Cast, save and share”, Edge under “Apps”).

It’s this same site, kept like an app: free, nothing from an app store, and it opens right to your plans.
